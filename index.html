VISUAL STORYTELLING

Mastering the craft.

I am a professional video editor specializing in making elegant property showcases. While studying Media Production and Design, I entered the field and learned the craft directly through hands-on experiences in the industry.

I use Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects, my expertise shines within my intentional cutting, framing, and song choice to keep viewer retention high.

I use carefully curated (copyright free) music tracks and audio mixing to elevate every listing, ensuring the atmosphere perfectly matches the feeling of the property.

My editing makes you feel as though you are walking through the home yourself, deliberately holding on special architectural moments and standout objects.

TECHNICAL EXPERTISE

Post-production precision.

Every video is meticulously crafted in post to make the property stand out among the rest. If you want to turn a standard walkthrough into a cinematic tour, I am ready to work with you.

I am highly comfortable processing C-Log 3 footage and color grading all camera formats to bring out the richest, most natural tones in a space.

I refine every cut to smoothly transition from shot to shot, ensuring clean transitions that preserve the flow of the video.

I stick to a 1-2 day turnaround time, my workflow is built for high-end results at industry speed.
